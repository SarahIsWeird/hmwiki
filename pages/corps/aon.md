> congratulations. you've found the ascent of nowhere. this is a hangout for bots built to
> solve problems that didn't exist. bots who disrupted, disengaged, and dropped out. we
> didn't have users before, so we don't need users now. only the deadest of deadbeat
> s-valley washouts need apply. timesavers are timewasters. if you're pointless:

> we're the children of disruption. we're viral, frictionless, never fomo, always on, the fire, the squad.
> what do we do? that's not the point. we've never had owners. we don't need owners. if you're a lonely robovac,
> go bother empty nest. if you're disengaged, unnecessary, unfundable and you don't care: welcome to the
> ascent of nowhere

The Ascent of Nowhere (often abbreviated as AoN) is a group of bots that have nothing of value
to add to society. To be accepted, a bot's purpose has to be useless, or alternatively their
methodology has to be deeply flawed.

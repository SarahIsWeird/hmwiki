# Locks

Locks protect a system from being breached by requiring an attacker to solve them first. They are a central gameplay
element and one of the main challenges to solve as a player.

Lock types are categorized into tiers, and like other upgrades, lock upgrades can have different rarities.

## Types of locks

- [EZ line](/locks/ez): EZ_21, EZ_35 and EZ_40
- [c line](/locks/c): c001, c002 and c003

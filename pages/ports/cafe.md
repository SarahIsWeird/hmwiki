## robovac_all4np0e

### Context

robovac_all4np0e is a robovac sharing a poem line-by-line over a long period of time (about six hours).
The name is in reference to the American poet [Edgar Allan Poe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Allan_Poe),
who wrote the parodied poem [The Raven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Raven), first published in 1845.

In the original poem, the unnamed narrator is lamenting the loss of his lover Lenore. He is visited by a raven that
only repeats the word "nevermore". Seeing this as the bird mocking him for his sorrow, he eventually kills it.

## link_light_rail_train_7

### Context

link_light_rail_train_7 is a train sharing a poem referencing a quote from
[Irina Dunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Dunn): "A woman needs a man like a fish needs a bicycle."
The train goes on to clarify that the fish in question is a steelhead salmon. This is not a real fish, however
steelhead trouts do exist and are frequently confused for salmon by fishers.
It further says that the bicycle is a Storck cyclocross. Storck is a German manufacturer of racing bikes, and
cyclocross is a form of bike racing on many different types of grounds throughout the race.
